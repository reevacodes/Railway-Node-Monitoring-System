ARP_IMPORTER.JS -> This file takes the ARP table data extracted from your Sophos Firewall, cleans and validates it, compares it with your registered MAC inventory, and saves the results in PostgreSQL.

parseCSVLine()	    -> Separates CSV columns
parseCSVOrCLI()	-> Reads CSV or CLI neighbor output
parseArpSnapshot()	-> Parses, cleans and validates ARP data
importArpSnapshot()	-> Saves the snapshot and discoveries
getLatestSnapshotSummary()	-> Retrieves the latest snapshot summary
getSnapshotDetails()	->Retrieves individual snapshot details
syncDiscoveredDevicesFromSnapshot()	-> Updates registered IPs and synchronizes discovered devices

Notice that the first three functions prepare data, the importer saves it, and the final functions retrieve or synchronize it.
